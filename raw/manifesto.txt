We love writing software. 

We think of good code as art. 

We love the craft and take pleasure in it. 

We thrive on the satisfaction we get from reaching goals. It's okay for something to reach version 1.0 and be complete. Software, like companies, don't need growth to be successful. 

Small problems are fun and small problems can have well written solutions that don't require dedicated support teams to keep alive. 

We believe doing something right is the only way that's worth doing anything.

We'll never get big because we care, because we don't need to and because we don't want to.
Getting big means abstraction that disconnect us from one another and we want the connection. 

We'll never sell something that we didn't write ourselves. We don't want AI having all the fun. We want to experience the joy and pleasure of crafting the intricate details of solutions.

Enough of this? What does it all mean? We're not sure yet. We might build some software that we want for ourselves and sell that to you if you're into it too. Do you have a need for something small but don't have the time or skills and our methods sound exciting and inspiring to you? Let's talk. We might be into it and writing something for you could be great.
